About

I work where investigation, technology, and human judgment meet.

My background spans criminological research, quantitative analysis, and software engineering. That combination shapes how I approach analytical problems: establish what the evidence actually shows, investigate inconsistencies, document the reasoning, and avoid treating automated outputs as ground truth.

I am especially interested in work where data and technology help analysts investigate complex behavior while preserving the need for careful human judgment.
